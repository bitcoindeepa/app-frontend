# Welcome to Bitcoin Deepa

Bitcoin Deepa (Bitcoin දීප) is a bitcoin membership app for Sri Lanka. You pay in rupees (LKR) and receive bitcoin, either automatically through a weekly or monthly plan or with a one-time buy. It runs inside Telegram as a Mini App, and also works in any web browser at [app.bitcoindeepa.com](https://app.bitcoindeepa.com), where you can install it like a normal app.

This guide covers everything from opening the app for the first time to selling your sats back to rupees.

## What you can do

- **Hold a bitcoin wallet** and see its value in sats, BTC, LKR or USD.
- **Subscribe to a plan** that buys bitcoin for you every week or every month.
- **Buy bitcoin once**, whenever you like, and pay with PayHere.
- **Sell sats** and receive rupees in your Sri Lankan bank account.
- **Send sats** to other members by their Bitcoin Deepa @username.
- **Track every payment, sale and transfer** in one activity feed.
- **Convert** between LKR, USD, BTC and sats with the built-in calculator.
- **Read bitcoin news** without leaving the app.

## What you need

- A Telegram account.
- A phone with a camera, to verify your identity.
- A National ID (NIC), passport or driving licence.
- A Sri Lankan bank account that you can pay from through PayHere.
- A bank account in your own name, if you want to sell sats for rupees.

## Start here

| Step | What you do                                                   | Time                                 |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 1    | [Open Bitcoin Deepa](installation/open-the-app.md)            | A few seconds                        |
| 2    | [Verify your identity](setup/verify-your-identity.md)         | About 2 minutes, plus up to 24 hours |
| 3    | [Set up your username](setup/set-up-your-username.md)         | A few seconds                        |
| 4    | [Choose a plan](setup/choose-a-plan.md) or buy once           | A few minutes                        |
| 5    | [Add a bank account](setup/add-a-bank-account.md), optional   | A few minutes, plus a few hours      |
| 6    | [Lock the app with a passkey](setup/passkey.md), in a browser | A few seconds                        |

The **Get started** card on your wallet tracks four of these for you (verifying, getting your first sats, adding a bank account and setting up your username) and disappears once they are all done.

You can look around the wallet before you verify. Plans, buying, selling and bank accounts unlock once your identity is approved.
