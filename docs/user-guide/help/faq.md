# FAQ

## Getting started

### Do I need to download anything?

No. Bitcoin Deepa opens inside Telegram from [@BitcoinDeepaBot](https://t.me/BitcoinDeepaBot). You can also use it in a web browser at [app.bitcoindeepa.com](https://app.bitcoindeepa.com) and, if you like, [add it to your home screen](../installation/install-on-your-phone.md).

### Do I need a password?

No. You sign in with your Telegram account, both in Telegram and in a browser. In a browser you can also [sign in with a passkey](../setup/passkey.md).

### Can I use it without verifying my identity?

You can open the app, look at the wallet and news, claim a username and send sats. Subscribing to a plan, buying bitcoin, selling sats and adding a bank account all need a [verified identity](../setup/verify-your-identity.md).

### Why do you need my ID?

Buying and selling bitcoin for rupees involves payment and banking rules, so the service has to confirm who its members are. Your data is encrypted, never shared, and used only to verify you.

## Plans and buying

### What is the difference between a plan and a one-time buy?

A [plan](../setup/choose-a-plan.md) charges you automatically every week or month. A [one-time buy](../usage/buy-bitcoin.md) is a single purchase you make when you choose. You can use either, or both.

### How do I pay?

Through PayHere, in Sri Lankan rupees. Plans are paid from your bank through PayHere. The app sends you to PayHere's page to pay and brings you back afterwards.

### How much bitcoin will I get?

That depends on the bitcoin price at the moment your payment is confirmed. The app shows an estimate before you pay, and the exact amount and price afterwards in [Activity](../usage/activity.md).

### Are there fees?

Yes. The fee is always shown before you confirm a buy, a plan or a sale. Sales may also have a fixed bank charge. For plans, your total fees are shown next to **You invested** in the wallet.

### Can I have more than one plan?

No, one at a time. To switch, cancel your current plan and pick a new one. See [Manage your plan](../usage/manage-your-plan.md).

### Can I cancel my plan?

Yes, at any time, from **Plans → Cancel plan**. Cancelling takes effect immediately.

### I cancelled my plan. What happens to my bitcoin?

It stays in your wallet. Cancelling only stops future payments.

## Selling

### How do I turn my bitcoin into rupees?

Use [Sell](../usage/sell-sats.md) on the Wallet tab. You need a verified [bank account](../setup/add-a-bank-account.md), and the rupees are sent there.

### Why do I have to upload a bank statement?

To confirm the account belongs to you. The statement is checked against your verified identity before the first payout.

### Why do I have to approve the payment after I sell?

Pressing and holding locks in the sale. Approving the payment moves the sats out of your wallet, so nothing leaves your wallet without your say-so. If you don't approve it in time, the sale is cancelled and nothing is sold.

### What if the bank transfer fails?

Your sats are returned to your wallet automatically and the sale is marked **Refunded**.

## Wallet and sending

### What are sats?

Sats, short for satoshis, are the smallest unit of bitcoin. One bitcoin is 100,000,000 sats. See the [Glossary](glossary.md).

### Why does my balance in rupees keep changing?

Your amount of bitcoin stays the same, but the price of bitcoin moves all the time, so its value in rupees moves with it.

### Why can't I sell or send all of my sats?

Some of them are probably in savings pots, which you set up in the wallet bot. They count in your total but stay locked until you withdraw them in the bot. Tap the ⓘ next to **You have … sats movable** to see the breakdown.

### How do I hide my balance?

Tap the eye icon on the Wallet, Activity or Plans tab, or turn on **Hide balances** in Profile & settings.

### Can I send bitcoin to a friend?

Yes, to any member by their Bitcoin Deepa @username. See [Send sats](../usage/send-sats.md). Gifting a plan to a friend is marked **Coming soon**.

### Is my wallet safe if someone picks up my phone?

In a browser or the installed app, [lock it with a passkey](../setup/passkey.md). Every send and sale payment in the browser also needs your passkey. Inside Telegram, use Telegram's own passcode lock.
