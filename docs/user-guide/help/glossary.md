# Glossary

| Term                  | Meaning                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bitcoin (BTC)**     | A digital currency. BTC is its unit.                                                                                        |
| **Sats (satoshis)**   | The smallest unit of bitcoin. 1 BTC = 100,000,000 sats. Most amounts in the app are in sats.                                |
| **LKR**               | Sri Lankan rupees, shown as රු. in the app.                                                                                 |
| **Wallet**            | Where your bitcoin balance is held and shown. The app and the wallet bot share the same wallet.                             |
| **Movable sats**      | The sats you can sell or send right now: your balance minus savings pots and payments still being sent.                     |
| **Savings pot**       | A pot you set up in the wallet bot to put sats aside. They count in your balance but can't be sold or sent until withdrawn. |
| **Username**          | Your Bitcoin Deepa @username, which others use to send you sats. Separate from your Telegram username.                      |
| **Plan**              | A membership that charges a fixed rupee amount every week or month and adds bitcoin to your wallet each time.               |
| **DCA**               | "Dollar-cost averaging": buying a fixed amount on a schedule, whatever the price. That's what a plan does.                  |
| **Membership Reward** | The bitcoin added to your wallet by a plan payment.                                                                         |
| **One-time buy**      | A single bitcoin purchase, separate from any plan.                                                                          |
| **KYC**               | "Know your customer". The identity check you complete once, with an ID document and a selfie.                               |
| **PayHere**           | The Sri Lankan payment service used for plan payments and one-time buys.                                                    |
| **Quote**             | A rate that is held for a short time when you sell, so you know exactly how many rupees you will receive.                   |
| **Fee**               | The charge on a buy, a plan payment or a sale. It is always shown before you confirm.                                       |
| **Bank charge**       | A fixed charge for the bank transfer when you sell, shown on the quote.                                                     |
| **Passkey**           | Face ID, your fingerprint or your device PIN, used to lock the app and approve payments in a browser.                       |
| **Average price**     | The average bitcoin price across all your purchases.                                                                        |
| **Chargeback**        | A payment that your bank reversed after it was made.                                                                        |
| **Invoice**           | A payment request you create with the wallet bot so that someone can send you sats.                                         |
| **Mini App**          | An app that runs inside Telegram. Bitcoin Deepa is one.                                                                     |
| **PWA**               | The installed web app: Bitcoin Deepa added to your home screen from a browser.                                              |
| **Lightning**         | A network for sending bitcoin quickly and cheaply. The wallet bot uses it for transfers.                                    |
