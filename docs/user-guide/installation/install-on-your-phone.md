# Install it on your phone

If you use Bitcoin Deepa in a web browser, you can add it to your home screen so it opens like a normal app. This is optional, and it is not needed inside Telegram.

You can install it from two places:

- The sign-in screen, with **Install the app**.
- **Profile & settings** (tap your picture at the top right), with **Install the app** near the bottom.

## Android and desktop (Chrome, Edge)

1. Open [app.bitcoindeepa.com](https://app.bitcoindeepa.com) in Chrome.
2. Tap **Install the app**.
3. Confirm in the dialog that the browser shows.

Bitcoin Deepa now has its own icon on your home screen.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Open [app.bitcoindeepa.com](https://app.bitcoindeepa.com) in Safari.
2. Tap **Install the app**. The app shows **Install Bitcoin Deepa** with these same steps.
3. Tap the **Share** button in your browser.
4. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.

## Good to know

- **Install the app** only appears in a browser that supports installing, and disappears once the app is installed.
- The installed app updates itself. You never need to reinstall it to get a new version.
- If you lose your connection, a banner says **You're offline. Showing the last data we had. Payments are paused.** Reconnect and pull down to refresh.
- Once installed, consider [locking the app with a passkey](../setup/passkey.md).
