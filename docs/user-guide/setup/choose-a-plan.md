# Choose a plan

A plan is a membership that charges you a fixed amount in rupees every week or every month and adds bitcoin to your wallet each time. You can cancel whenever you like.

{% hint style="info" %}
You need to [verify your identity](verify-your-identity.md) first. Until then, the **Plans** tab shows **Verification required** with a **Complete verification** button.
{% endhint %}

A plan is optional. If you would rather buy now and then, skip this page and use a [one-time buy](../usage/buy-bitcoin.md).

## Steps

1. Open the **Plans** tab. With no active plan, it shows **Choose your plan**.
2. Pick **Weekly** or **Monthly** with the switch at the top.
3. Tap a plan to select it. The card opens to show roughly how many sats each payment buys at today's rate, and the fee per payment.
4. Tap **Activate**, the button at the bottom that names the plan and its price.
5. Check the **Confirm plan** sheet and tap **Continue to PayHere**.
6. Pay on the PayHere page that opens.

<!-- screenshot: "Choose your plan" with one plan selected and expanded -->

Each plan shows its name and its price in LKR per week or per month. One plan may carry a **Popular** badge.

### The Confirm plan sheet

| Row           | Meaning                                                     |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pays with** | **PayHere · bank**: you pay from your bank through PayHere. |
| **Billed**    | **Every week** or **Every month**.                          |
| **Fee**       | The fee, as a percentage of each payment.                   |
| **Cancel**    | **Any time, in Plans**.                                     |

## After you pay

| You see                | What happened                                                                                               |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Payment successful** | Your plan is active. Tap **Go to wallet**.                                                                  |
| **Payment cancelled**  | The payment was not completed and nothing changed. Tap **Back to plans** to try again, or **Go to wallet**. |

Once your plan is active:

- The **Wallet** tab shows it under **My plan** with a green **Active** badge, the date of your **Last reward** and your **Next reward**.
- The **Plans** tab shows **Manage my plan**. See [Manage your plan](../usage/manage-your-plan.md).
- Each payment appears in the **Activity** tab as a **Membership Reward**.

## What it costs

The price on the plan card is what you are charged each period. The fee is taken from each payment, and the rest buys bitcoin. Your total fees are shown in your wallet next to **You invested**, so you can always see how much went into bitcoin and how much went to fees.

{% hint style="info" %}
You can have one plan at a time. To switch plans, see [Manage your plan](../usage/manage-your-plan.md).
{% endhint %}
