# Lock the app with a passkey

In a web browser or the installed app, you can protect Bitcoin Deepa with a passkey: Face ID, your fingerprint or your device PIN. A passkey does three things:

- **Locks the app.** Bitcoin Deepa asks for your passkey every time you open it, and again after it has been in the background for a minute.
- **Approves every send and sale payment** you make in the browser. This is required, so the app asks you to set up a passkey the first time you send or sell.
- **Lets you sign in** with **Sign in with passkey**, without going through Telegram.

{% hint style="info" %}
Passkeys only work in the browser and the installed app. Inside Telegram, use Telegram's own passcode lock instead: in Telegram, go to **Settings › Privacy and Security**.
{% endhint %}

## Set up a passkey

1. Tap your picture at the top right to open **Profile & settings**.
2. Under **App lock**, tap **Set up** next to **Lock with a passkey**.
3. Approve the prompt from your device with Face ID, your fingerprint or your PIN.

You see **Passkey lock on**. From now on the app asks for your passkey when you open it.

You can also set one up the first time you send or sell: the app shows **Set up a passkey**. Tap **Set up passkey** to do it there, or **Not now** to stop without sending anything.

## Add or remove passkeys

Under **App lock** you see each passkey with the date it was added or last used.

- **Add another passkey**: adds one for another phone or computer.
- The bin icon next to a passkey removes it, after you confirm with **Remove**. You can't unlock with that passkey afterwards.

Removing your last passkey turns the lock off: you see **Passkey lock off**, and the app stops asking for one.

## Unlocking

When the app is locked, your device's passkey prompt opens straight away. If you close it, you see **Bitcoin Deepa is locked** with:

- **Unlock with passkey**: try again.
- **Can't use your passkey?**: help if you lost the device with your passkey.
- **Log out**: signs you out of this browser.

## Approving payments

When you send sats or pay for a sale in the browser, your device asks for your passkey just before the payment goes out. If you close the prompt or it fails, nothing is sent or sold, and you can try again.

## Lost your passkey?

Message support in the [Bitcoin Deepa bot](https://t.me/BitcoinDeepaBot). On the locked screen, tap **Can't use your passkey?** and then **Message support**. Once the team has checked it's you, they remove the lost passkey and you can add a new one.
