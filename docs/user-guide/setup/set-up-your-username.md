# Set up your username

Your Bitcoin Deepa username is how other members find you to [send you sats](../usage/send-sats.md). It is yours inside Bitcoin Deepa, separate from your Telegram username, and you don't need to be verified to claim one.

## Steps

1. Open the username sheet. Either:
   - tap **Set up your username** on the **Get started** card on your wallet, or
   - open [Profile & settings](../usage/profile.md) and tap **Set up** next to **BitcoinDeepa username**.
2. Type the username you want. The app checks it as you type and shows **@name is available** when it is free.
3. Tap **Claim @name**.

You see **You're @name**, and the username appears under your name at the top of the app.

<!-- screenshot: the "Your username" sheet with an available name -->

## Rules

- 3 to 20 characters: letters, digits or underscores.
- It must start with a letter.
- Capital letters are ignored, so `@Kasun_77` and `@kasun_77` are the same name.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Choose carefully. Once you claim a username, you can't change it later.
{% endhint %}

## Share it

In Profile & settings, the copy button next to **BitcoinDeepa username** copies your `@username`, ready to paste into a message.
