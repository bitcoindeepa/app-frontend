# Verify your identity

Before you can subscribe to a plan, buy bitcoin, sell sats or add a bank account, Bitcoin Deepa needs to confirm who you are. This is often called KYC ("know your customer"). You do it once.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- **A phone with a camera.** Verification has to be done on a mobile device.
- **One identity document:** your NIC, passport or driving licence. You photograph the front and back.
- **Good lighting**, and nothing covering your face. Remove hats and sunglasses for the selfie.

Filling in the form and taking the photos takes less than two minutes.

## Steps

1. Open the verification screen. Any of these takes you there:
   - **Verify your identity** on the **Get started** card on your wallet.
   - **Verify** next to **Identity** in [Profile & settings](../usage/profile.md).
   - **Verify** on the notice in Buy and Sell, or **Complete verification** in the **Plans** tab. Both show until you are verified.
2. Read the steps on **Verify your identity** and tap **Start verification**.
3. Fill in **Your details** and tap **Start verification** again.
4. On the verification page that opens, photograph your identity document.
5. Take a selfie.
6. Wait for the review.

### Your details

Use the name exactly as it is on your ID.

| Field          | What to enter                                                         |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **First name** | As it appears on your identity document.                              |
| **Last name**  | As it appears on your identity document.                              |
| **Email**      | An address you check.                                                 |
| **Phone**      | A Sri Lankan number, such as `0771234567`. `+94771234567` also works. |
| **Address**    | Your street address, at least 5 characters.                           |
| **City**       | For example, Colombo.                                                 |
| **Country**    | Filled in as Sri Lanka. Change it if needed.                          |

The **Start verification** button stays disabled until every field is valid.

## While you wait

After you submit, the screen shows **We're reviewing your documents** with an **In review** badge. A review usually takes up to 24 hours, and you get a notification when it is done. Tap **Back to home** and keep using the wallet in the meantime.

You can check progress at any time under **Identity** in Profile & settings.

| Identity says                                | What it means                                        | What to do                                        |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Needed to buy plans and sell sats**        | You have not begun verification.                     | Tap **Verify**, then **Start verification**.      |
| **Started. Finish it to buy and sell**       | You began but have not finished the photos.          | Tap **Continue**, then **Continue verification**. |
| **In review**                                | Your documents were submitted and are being checked. | Wait. This usually takes up to 24 hours.          |
| **Verified**                                 | You are approved.                                    | Nothing. The app takes you to plans.              |
| **Declined. You can try again**              | The documents could not be accepted.                 | Tap **Verify**, then **Start new verification**.  |
| **Expired. Please verify again**             | The session, or an older approval, has run out.      | Tap **Verify**, then **Start new verification**.  |
| **Not finished. Pick up where you left off** | Verification was abandoned part-way.                 | Tap **Verify**, then **Start new verification**.  |

When you are approved, the verification screen says **You're verified** and moves on to the plans after a moment. Your picture at the top of the app also gets a green tick.

## If you are on a computer

Tapping **Start verification** on a desktop opens **Use your phone**, because identity verification needs a phone camera. To continue:

1. Open Bitcoin Deepa on your phone, in Telegram or your mobile browser.
2. Sign in with the same Telegram account.
3. Start the verification from there.
