# Buy bitcoin

A one-time buy lets you buy bitcoin whenever you like, with or without a plan. You pay in rupees through PayHere and the sats go straight to your Bitcoin Deepa wallet.

{% hint style="info" %}
You need to [verify your identity](../setup/verify-your-identity.md) before you can buy. Until then, the Buy screen shows a notice that takes you there, and you can't continue.
{% endhint %}

## Steps

1. On the **Wallet** tab, tap **Buy**. The **Buy bitcoin** sheet opens. (The **Buy** and **Sell** switch at the top lets you change your mind.)
2. Under **You pay**, enter the amount in rupees, or tap one of the quick amounts.
3. Check **You get (estimate)** and the line underneath, which shows the current price and fee.
4. Tap **Review**.
5. Check **Review purchase** and tap **Pay … with PayHere**.
6. Complete the payment on PayHere's page.

## What the screens tell you

On the amount screen:

- **You get (estimate)**: roughly how many sats you will receive.
- **From … · up to … at a time**: the smallest and largest amount for a single buy.
- **1 BTC ≈ … · …% fee**: the current bitcoin price in LKR and the fee.

On **Review purchase**:

| Row            | Meaning                                                                                           |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **You pay**    | The rupees you pay.                                                                               |
| **Fees**       | The fee, as a percentage and in rupees. It is taken from what you pay, and the rest buys bitcoin. |
| **Rate**       | The bitcoin price in LKR.                                                                         |
| **Pay with**   | PayHere.                                                                                          |
| **Sats go to** | Your Bitcoin Deepa wallet.                                                                        |

Under the rows is the most you can buy in a day.

{% hint style="info" %}
**This is an estimate.** The bitcoin price is set when PayHere confirms your payment, so you may get a few more or fewer sats than shown.
{% endhint %}

## After you pay

PayHere returns you to Bitcoin Deepa, which shows the status of your purchase and a timeline of its steps. The page updates by itself.

| You see                           | What it means                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Waiting for PayHere**           | PayHere is confirming your payment. This usually takes a few seconds.                                      |
| **Payment pending**               | PayHere is still processing your payment.                                                                  |
| **Payment received**              | You are paid up and your sats are being sent to your wallet.                                               |
| **Bitcoin received**              | Done. The sats are in your wallet.                                                                         |
| **Payment under review**          | Your payment arrived and the team is checking it before sending your sats. This usually takes a few hours. |
| **Payment cancelled**             | You left the checkout without paying, or the payment was cancelled. Nothing was charged.                   |
| **Payment failed**                | The payment did not go through. Nothing was charged.                                                       |
| **No payment received**           | The checkout ran out of time with no payment.                                                              |
| **Still confirming your payment** | PayHere has not confirmed yet. You can leave the page.                                                     |
| **Payment reversed**              | Your bank reversed the payment. Contact support if you did not expect this.                                |

You do not have to keep the status page open. Once a payment is confirmed, the purchase appears in the **Activity** tab as a **Bitcoin Purchase**, and the sats show in your wallet.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you paid and the status is still unclear, do not pay again. A confirmed payment always shows up in **Activity**. Check there first.
{% endhint %}

## Buy again

Tap **Go to wallet** to return to your wallet. If a purchase was cancelled, failed or got no payment, the page also offers **Buy again**. To buy more after a successful purchase, tap **Buy** on the wallet.
