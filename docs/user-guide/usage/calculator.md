# Calculator

The calculator converts between rupees, US dollars, bitcoin and sats at the app's current price.

To open it, tap the calculator icon at the bottom right of the **Total value** card on the **Wallet** tab.

## Use it

The calculator has four boxes: **Sri Lankan rupees**, **US dollars**, **Bitcoin** and **Satoshis**. Type an amount in any one of them and the other three update straight away. Quick amounts in rupees are listed underneath.

Under the boxes, **Rates** shows the current price of **1 BTC** in LKR and in USD, the price of **1 USD** in LKR, and when the rates were last updated.

## Good to know

- The calculator is for working out amounts only. It doesn't buy or sell anything.
- If the US dollar price isn't available, the **US dollars** box says **Unavailable**. The other boxes still work.
- If the app can't get the bitcoin price, the calculator says so. Tap **Try again**.
