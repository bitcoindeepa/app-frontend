# Manage your plan

Once you have a plan, the **Plans** tab is where you check on it, switch to a different one, or cancel it.

Don't have a plan yet? See [Choose a plan](../setup/choose-a-plan.md).

## See how your plan is doing

Open the **Plans** tab. The **Manage my plan** card shows:

- Your plan's name, its price per week or per month, and an **Active** badge.
- **You invested**: the rupees that went into bitcoin, the sats you received, and any fees.
- **Current value**: what that bitcoin is worth now, with your profit or loss.
- **Subscribed since** and **Next reward** dates.

Tap the eye icon to hide the amounts.

## Switch to a different plan

You can have one plan at a time, so switching means cancelling your current plan first.

1. On the **Plans** tab, tap **Change plan**.
2. Read **Cancel your plan first?** and tap **Cancel** with your plan's name to go ahead, or **Keep it** to back out.
3. You see **Plan cancelled. Now pick your new plan.** The Plans tab turns into the plan picker.
4. Pick your new plan and pay, as in [Choose a plan](../setup/choose-a-plan.md).

## Cancel your plan

1. On the **Plans** tab, tap **Cancel plan**.
2. Confirm with **Cancel plan**, or tap **Keep it** to back out.

You see **Plan cancelled**, and the Plans tab shows the plan picker again.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Cancelling takes effect immediately and cannot be undone: your plan stops buying bitcoin right away. The sats already in your wallet stay yours. To start again later, choose a plan as you did the first time.
{% endhint %}
