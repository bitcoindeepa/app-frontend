# Profile & settings

**Profile & settings** holds your picture, username, verification status, app lock, bank account and personal details.

To open it, tap your picture at the top right of any tab.

<!-- screenshot: the Profile & settings sheet -->

## Your profile

The top row shows your picture, your name, your @username and a **Verified** or **Not verified** badge.

### Change your picture

1. Tap your picture. The pencil on its corner shows it can be changed.
2. Pick a picture from the grid. Tap **Show me more** for new ones.
3. Tap **Use this picture**.

You see **Profile picture updated**.

## Appearance

Choose **Auto** (follows your device), **Light** or **Dark**.

## Account

| Row                       | What it does                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Identity**              | Your verification status. Tap **Verify** or **Continue** to start or finish. See [Verify your identity](../setup/verify-your-identity.md). |
| **BitcoinDeepa username** | Tap **Set up** to claim one, or the copy button to copy yours. See [Set up your username](../setup/set-up-your-username.md).               |
| **Hide balances**         | Masks amounts on screen everywhere in the app. Same as the eye icon.                                                                       |

## App lock

In a browser or the installed app, **Lock with a passkey** turns on the passkey lock and lists your passkeys. Inside Telegram this section points you to Telegram's own passcode lock instead. See [Lock the app with a passkey](../setup/passkey.md).

## Payout account

The bank account your rupees are paid into when you [sell sats](sell-sats.md), with its status: **In review**, **Verified** or **Rejected**.

- No account yet? Tap **Add bank account**.
- To change it, remove it with the bin icon, then add the new one.

See [Add a bank account](../setup/add-a-bank-account.md) for the full steps. You need to be verified before you can add one.

## Personal details

Your **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **City**, as you entered them for verification.

If these are empty, tap **Complete your profile** to fill them in.

## Install the app

In a browser that supports it, **Install the app** adds Bitcoin Deepa to your home screen. See [Install it on your phone](../installation/install-on-your-phone.md).

## Log out

- **Log out** (browser only) signs you out of this browser.
- **Log out of all devices** ends every session, on every phone and browser. It is there inside Telegram too.

If you see **Couldn't log out**, check your connection and try again. You're still logged in.
