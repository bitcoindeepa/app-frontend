# Send sats

You can send sats to any other Bitcoin Deepa member by their Bitcoin Deepa @username. The sats move between wallets straight away.

## Steps

1. On the **Wallet** tab, tap **Send**. The **Send sats** sheet opens.
2. Under **To**, type the person's Bitcoin Deepa username. When the app finds them, it shows their name and picture with a tick. People you have sent to before are listed underneath, so you can tap one instead.
3. Enter the amount under **You send**, in sats, or tap the swap button to type it in rupees. The quick buttons fill in **1,000**, **5,000** or **10,000 sats**, or **Max**.
4. Tap **Review**.
5. Check the details and add a **Note** if you like, such as what it's for.
6. **Press and hold** the **Send … sats** button until it completes. In a browser, your device then asks for your [passkey](../setup/passkey.md).

<!-- screenshot: the Send sats sheet with a recipient found -->

The sheet shows **Sent** when the sats arrive. Tap **Done**, or **View payment** to see its details.

{% hint style="warning" %}
A send can't be undone. Check the username and the amount before you press and hold.
{% endhint %}

## What the screens tell you

- **You have … sats movable**: the sats you can send now. Tap the ⓘ for the breakdown. Sats in savings pots can't be sent until you withdraw them in the bot.
- **Up to … sats per send · … more today**: your limits for one send and for the rest of the day.
- **Left today after this**, on the review: how much more you can send today after this payment.

## Larger payments

Above a certain amount, the review says **You'll confirm this payment in the Bitcoin Deepa bot chat.** After you press and hold, the bot sends you a message. Tap **Confirm** there to send the payment, or **Cancel** if you didn't start it.

## Payment status

| You see                     | What it means                                             |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sending**                 | Your payment is on its way. This only takes a moment.     |
| **Confirm in the bot chat** | Waiting for you to tap **Confirm** in the bot.            |
| **Almost there**            | The payment is on its way. It won't be sent twice.        |
| **Sent**                    | Delivered.                                                |
| **Payment cancelled**       | It was cancelled in the bot chat. Nothing was sent.       |
| **Not confirmed in time**   | It wasn't confirmed in the bot in time. Nothing was sent. |
| **Payment failed**          | Nothing was sent.                                         |

If the app says **We couldn't confirm this payment yet**, hold **Send** again to check. It won't send the payment twice.

Sends appear in the **Activity** tab as **Sent**, and sats you receive as **Received**.

## Receive sats

To let people send to you, [set up your username](../setup/set-up-your-username.md) and share it. If you haven't claimed one yet, the Send sheet shows a link to do it.
