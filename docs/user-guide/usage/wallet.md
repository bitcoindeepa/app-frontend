# Wallet

The **Wallet** tab is the home screen. It shows how much bitcoin you hold, what it is worth, and how your plan is doing.

<!-- screenshot: the full Wallet tab -->

## Top bar

Your name and @username are at the top right, next to your picture. Tap the picture to open [Profile & settings](profile.md). A green tick on it means your identity is verified.

## Total value

The large card shows your whole balance.

- **Tap the big number** to switch it between sats and money. The smaller line underneath shows the same balance in BTC and in the other unit.
- **LKR / USD**: the button at the top of the card switches money amounts between rupees and US dollars, across the wallet. Money amounts are marked `≈` because they move with the bitcoin price.
- **Eye icon**: hides or shows every amount. Useful when someone is looking over your shoulder.
- **Last 24h**: how much your balance's value changed over the last day, in money and as a percentage. Green means up, red means down.
- **Calculator icon** (bottom right): opens the [calculator](calculator.md).

If you have sats in savings pots (set up in the wallet bot), the card also shows how many are **in savings pots**, with a lock. They count in your total, but they can't be sold or sent until you withdraw them in the bot.

If the card says **Balance unavailable**, the app couldn't reach your wallet just now. Try again in a few minutes, or pull down to refresh.

## Quick actions

Under the balance are four buttons:

| Button      | What it does                                                 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Buy**     | A one-time purchase. See [Buy bitcoin](buy-bitcoin.md).      |
| **Sell**    | Turns sats into rupees. See [Sell sats](sell-sats.md).       |
| **Send**    | Sends sats to another member. See [Send sats](send-sats.md). |
| **Request** | Coming soon.                                                 |

## Get started

Until you have finished setting up, a **Get started** card lists four steps, with a progress bar. Tap a step to do it:

1. **Verify your identity**. See [Verify your identity](../setup/verify-your-identity.md).
2. **Get your first sats**: opens Buy.
3. **Add a bank account**. See [Add a bank account](../setup/add-a-bank-account.md).
4. **Set up your username**. See [Set up your username](../setup/set-up-your-username.md).

Finished steps are ticked off. The card disappears once all four are done.

## My plan

Your current plan, its price, and an **Active** badge. Two smaller cards show:

- **Last reward**: the date of your latest plan payment, or **None yet**.
- **Next reward**: the date of your next plan payment.

With no plan, this section says **No active DCA plan**. Tap the plan row either way to open the **Plans** tab.

## Performance

Four cards summarise your results:

| Card              | What it shows                                                                           |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **You invested**  | The money that went into bitcoin, the bitcoin you received for it, and any fees on top. |
| **Current value** | What that bitcoin is worth now, with your profit or loss in money and as a percentage.  |
| **Average price** | The average bitcoin price you bought at, in LKR and USD.                                |
| **Current price** | The bitcoin price right now.                                                            |

Your profit or loss is measured against everything you paid, fees included.

## Reward portfolio performance

A chart of your purchases over time. Pick **3M**, **1Y** or **All** to change the period, and tap or hover over the chart to see the values on a given date.

| Line          | Meaning                                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Portfolio** | The value of your bitcoin at today's price. |
| **Invested**  | The total rupees that went into bitcoin.    |
| **Bitcoin**   | The bitcoin price at each purchase.         |

When **Portfolio** is above **Invested**, you are in profit. Before your first purchase, the chart says **No rewards yet**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The bitcoin price goes down as well as up. The rupee values on this screen are estimates at the current price, not a guaranteed amount.
{% endhint %}
